Meet Desktop Block

Desktop Block is a virtual desktop companion who lives on your desktop and keeps you company throughout the day.

Desktop Block can talk, jump around, sleep, remember a name you give him, and share random thoughts while he explores your desktop.

About Desktop Block

"I am a block made by Team Ruby, and I was made to be your friend. -desktop block"

Desktop Block is designed to be a fun companion rather than a productivity tool. He spends most of his time hopping around your desktop, sharing thoughts, and occasionally forgetting his own name.
